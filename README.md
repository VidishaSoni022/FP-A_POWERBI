📊 99 Agency — FP&A Budget Model (Excel)
A full Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) model built in Excel for 99 Agency, a digital marketing agency offering Ad and SEO campaign services. The model covers historical actuals for FY2023–FY2024 and a fully integrated FY2025 budget.

🏢 Company Overview
99 Agency is a digital marketing firm generating revenue through two service lines — Ad Campaigns and SEO Campaigns — across a client base including Client A, B, C, and Other segments.

📁 Workbook Structure
| **Sheet** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Income Statement** | Quarterly P&L: Revenue → Gross Profit → EBITDA → Net Income (FY2023–FY2024 actuals) |
| **Balance Sheet** | Quarterly snapshot of Assets, Liabilities & Equity with DSO/DPO metrics |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Indirect method cash flow for FY2024 |
| **Revenue – Bottom-Up** | Client-level volume × price model for Ad & SEO campaigns with YoY variance |
| **Revenue – Top-Down** | Market-sizing approach: population → internet users → agency revenue → market share |
| **Revenue Budget** | Reconciliation of bottom-up vs. top-down projections; final FY2025 budget |
| **Cost of Sales – Ad** | Personnel count × salary + campaign volume × unit cost |
| **Cost of Sales – SEO** | Same driver-based model for SEO delivery team |
| **SG&A** | Fixed fees (rent, legal, management) + variable commissions (10% of revenue) |
| **Fixed Assets** | CAPEX schedule with D&A as % of opening asset value |
| **Working Capital** | DSO/DPO-driven Trade Receivables & Payables forecasts |
| **Financial Liabilities** | Debt balance and quarterly interest expense at fixed rate |
| **IS (Master Budget)** | Integrated Income Statement — actuals vs. FY2025 budget |
| **BS (Master Budget)** | Integrated Balance Sheet — actuals vs. FY2025 budget |

🧮 Modeling Approach
Revenue forecasting uses two methods:
**Bottom-Up**: Number of campaigns per client × average revenue per campaign, with growth assumptions at the client level
**Top-Down**: Market sizing from country population → internet users → agency fee pool → market share.

The final budget averages both approaches.
| **[Driver](ca://s?q=FP_and_A_cost_drivers)** | **[Formula](ca://s?q=FP_and_A_cost_formulas)** | **[Basis](ca://s?q=FP_and_A_model_basis)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cost of Sales** | Headcount × Salary + Campaign Volume × Unit Cost | Driven by staffing levels and campaign activity |
| **SG&A** | Fixed Line Items + (10% of Revenue as Selling Commissions) + (1% of Revenue as External Services) | Mix of fixed overhead and variable costs tied to revenue |
| **D&A** | % of Beginning Fixed Asset Value | Depreciation based on opening asset balances |
| **Interest** | Fixed Rate × Financial Liabilities Balance | Debt servicing cost |
| **Working Capital** | DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) + DPO (Days Payable Outstanding) averages | Derived from historical actuals |
